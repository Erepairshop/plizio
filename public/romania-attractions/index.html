Frequently asked questions

What are the top attractions in Romania?

Among the most popular are Techirghiol Lake, Măcin Mountains National Park, Jibou Botanical Garden, Sinoe Lake, Deva Fortress and many more – the full top 50 list is on this page.

When is the best time to visit Romania?

May to October offers the most pleasant weather; July and August are warmest and busiest.
