Frequently asked questions

What are the top attractions in Slovakia?

Among the most popular are Devínska Kobyla Nature Reserve, Arboretum Mlyňany, Slovak Karst, Latorica Protected Landscape Area, Šúr Nature Reserve and many more – the full top 50 list is on this page.

When is the best time to visit Slovakia?

May to October offers the most pleasant weather; July and August are warmest and busiest.
