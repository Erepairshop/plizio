Frequently asked questions

What are the top attractions in Greece?

Among the most popular are Samaria Gorge, Mount Olympus National Park, Lake Orestiada, Polylimnio Waterfalls, Edessa Waterfalls and many more – the full top 50 list is on this page.

When is the best time to visit Greece?

May to October offers the most pleasant weather; July and August are warmest and busiest.
