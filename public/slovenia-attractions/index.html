Frequently asked questions

What are the top attractions in Slovenia?

Among the most popular are Turjak Castle, Žužemberk Castle, Snežnik Castle, Bogenšperk Castle, Nebotičnik (Skyscraper) and many more – the full top 50 list is on this page.

When is the best time to visit Slovenia?

May to October offers the most pleasant weather; July and August are warmest and busiest.
