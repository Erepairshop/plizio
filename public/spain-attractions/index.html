Frequently asked questions

What are the top attractions in Spain?

Among the most popular are Timanfaya National Park, Royal Botanical Garden of Madrid, Cabrera Archipelago National Park, Caldera de Taburiente National Park, Marismas del Odiel and many more – the full top 50 list is on this page.

When is the best time to visit Spain?

May to October offers the most pleasant weather; July and August are warmest and busiest.
