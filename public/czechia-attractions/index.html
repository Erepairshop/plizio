Frequently asked questions

What are the top attractions in Czechia?

Among the most popular are Prague Stock Exchange, Tisa Walls, Palava Hills, Rip, Historic Centre of Telč and many more – the full top 50 list is on this page.

When is the best time to visit Czechia?

May to October offers the most pleasant weather; July and August are warmest and busiest.
