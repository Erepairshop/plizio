Prahova

Prahova is a county in the historical region of Muntenia, Romania, with its capital at Ploiești. It is one of the most urbanized and industrialized counties in the country, historically famous for its oil extraction and refining industry. The northern part of the county includes the scenic Bucegi Mountains and the popular Prahova Valley, a major tourist destination with resorts like Sinaia, Bușteni, and Azuga. Sinaia is home to the stunning Peleș Castle, a former royal residence. Prahova perfectly balances significant industrial economic power with outstanding natural and historical attractions.
