Satu Mare

Satu Mare is a county in the historical regions of Maramureș and Crișana, in northwestern Romania, bordering Hungary and Ukraine. Its capital city is also named Satu Mare. The county has a flat landscape, being part of the Pannonian Plain, and is crossed by the Someș River. It boasts a diverse cultural heritage, with significant Romanian, Hungarian, and Swabian communities. Historical attractions include the Károlyi Castle in Carei and the traditional pottery center in Vama.

The capital city is Satu Mare.It shares borders with both Hungary and Ukraine.The Someș River flows through the county and its capital.It has a very diverse population, including Romanians, Hungarians, and Swabians.
