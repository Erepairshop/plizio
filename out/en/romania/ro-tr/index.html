Teleorman

Teleorman is a county in the historical region of Muntenia, southern Romania, with its capital at Alexandria. It is situated on the Wallachian Plain and is bordered to the south by the Danube River, which separates it from Bulgaria. The county is primarily agricultural, known for its vast fields of wheat, corn, and sunflowers. It has a rural character, preserving many traditional Romanian customs and folklore. Despite being one of the less economically developed counties, it offers peaceful landscapes and important archaeological sites along the Danube.

Alexandria is the capital and largest city of the county.The county is located entirely in the historical region of Muntenia.The Danube River forms its southern border with Bulgaria.It is predominantly an agricultural county.
