Caraș-Severin

Caraș-Severin County is located in the south-western part of Romania, forming part of the historical region of Banat. It is the third-largest county in Romania by land area, renowned for its extensive mountainous terrain and pristine natural parks. The county seat is Reșița, a city with a rich history in the iron and steel industry. The region is highly attractive for ecotourism, featuring the stunning Nera Gorge-Beușnița National Park and the Semenic Mountains. Additionally, it shares a border with Serbia, delimited by the dramatic Danube Gorge.
