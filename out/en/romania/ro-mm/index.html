Maramureș

Maramureș County is located in the northwestern part of Romania, encompassing parts of the historical regions of Maramureș, Transylvania, and Crișana. Its administrative center is Baia Mare, a city with a long history of mining. The county is globally recognized for its well-preserved rural traditions, intricate wooden architecture, and the famous wooden churches of Maramureș. The landscape is dominated by the majestic Carpathian Mountains, making it a popular destination for nature lovers and hikers. It shares a northern border with Ukraine.

The county seat and largest city is Baia Mare.It shares an international border with Ukraine to the north.The total area of the county is 6,304 square kilometers.The Wooden Churches of Maramureș are UNESCO World Heritage sites.
