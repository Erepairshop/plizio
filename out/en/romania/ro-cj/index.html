Cluj-Napoca

Cluj-Napoca is the unofficial capital of Transylvania and a major hub for education, IT, and culture. The city is located in the Someș Valley at the edge of the Apuseni Mountains and boasts a history spanning over 2000 years. It is known for its vibrant atmosphere, numerous festivals, and an impressive blend of Gothic, Baroque, and modern architecture. As one of Romania's most dynamic cities, it attracts students and professionals from all over the country.
