Dâmbovița

Dâmbovița County is situated in the southern-central part of Romania, in the historical region of Wallachia. The county seat, Târgoviște, is steeped in history, having served as the capital of the Principality of Wallachia for several centuries. The northern part of the county features the majestic Bucegi Mountains, while the southern area consists of fertile plains suitable for agriculture. Dâmbovița is highly industrialized, with significant metallurgical, chemical, and heavy machinery plants. The region is also famous for the Chindia Tower, a symbol of the former royal court.
