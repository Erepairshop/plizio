Regions

Sibiu

Sibiu is a county in the historical region of Transylvania, Romania, with its capital at the city of Sibiu. It is renowned for its rich Saxon heritage, visible in the fortified churches and medieval architecture of its towns and villages. The city of Sibiu was the European Capital of Culture in 2007 and features well-preserved medieval walls, squares, and the Brukenthal National Museum. The southern part of the county is dominated by the Făgăraș and Cindrel Mountains, offering spectacular alpine scenery. The region is also famous for its traditional gastronomy and cheese production in the Mărginimea Sibiului area.

Cities

Sibiu

Sibiu is one of the most picturesque cities in Transylvania, famous for its exceptionally well-preserved historical center. Founded in the 12th century by German settlers, the city served for centuries as the cultural and political hub of the Transylvanian Saxons. With its three interconnected squares, characteristic houses with 'eyes', and medieval fortification systems, Sibiu exudes a unique European charm. Today, the city is a major cultural center, enjoying world renown through its international festivals and museums.
