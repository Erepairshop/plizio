Feldberg (Schwarzwald)

View on the map

The Feldberg in the Black Forest at 1493 m is the highest peak in Baden-Württemberg and in the entire Black Forest. It offers ski areas in winter and wonderful hiking trails in summer with views of the Alps and Lake Constance.

Facts

  • 1493 m – highest peak in Baden-Württemberg.
  • Ski area in winter.
  • Panorama of Alps and Lake Constance.

Schwarzwald

The Black Forest is Germany's largest contiguous mid-mountain range in Baden-Württemberg, known for dense forests, picturesque valleys, and traditional cuckoo clocks. It gives its name to the Black Forest gateau and is home to the fairy-tale world of the Brothers Grimm.
