Stuttgart

Stuttgart, the capital of Baden-Württemberg, is idyllically located in a valley basin surrounded by vineyards. It is known worldwide as a center of the automotive industry, characterized by brands like Mercedes-Benz and Porsche. In addition to its industrial strength, Stuttgart offers a high quality of life with many green spaces and cultural institutions. The city combines Swabian coziness with global technological innovation.
