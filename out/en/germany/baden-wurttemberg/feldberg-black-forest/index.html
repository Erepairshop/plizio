Feldberg (Black Forest)

View on the map

The Feldberg at 1,493 metres is the highest peak in the Black Forest and in all of Baden-Württemberg.

Facts

  • Highest point in Baden-Württemberg at 1,493 m.
  • Popular ski resort in winter.
  • The Feldberg nature reserve includes species-rich mountain meadows.
  • A weather station and a tower stand on the summit.
  • Clear views of the Alps possible on good days.

Feldberg (Schwarzwald)

At 1,493 meters, the Feldberg in Baden-Württemberg is the highest mountain in the Black Forest and the highest German mountain outside the Alps. The elevation offers spectacular panoramic views, which on clear days stretch as far as the Vosges in France and the Swiss Alps. The area surrounding the Feldberg is the largest and oldest nature reserve in Baden-Württemberg, characterized by rare plant and animal species. In winter, the region transforms into the most important winter sports center in the Black Forest, while in summer it is a paradise for hikers and nature lovers.

Stuttgart

Stuttgart, the capital of Baden-Württemberg, is idyllically located in a valley basin surrounded by vineyards. It is known worldwide as a center of the automotive industry, characterized by brands like Mercedes-Benz and Porsche. In addition to its industrial strength, Stuttgart offers a high quality of life with many green spaces and cultural institutions. The city combines Swabian coziness with global technological innovation.

Heidelberg

Heidelberg is considered the epitome of German Romanticism and charms with its picturesque location on the Neckar River. The historic Old Town remained largely undamaged during World War II and attracts millions of visitors annually. The cityscape is dominated by the imposing ruins of Heidelberg Castle. The city is also home to the oldest university in Germany, which gives it a youthful and international flair.

Freiburg im Breisgau

Freiburg im Breisgau is located in southwestern Baden-Württemberg and boasts of being the sunniest major city in Germany. Situated on the edge of the picturesque Black Forest, the city offers an extremely high quality of life. The historic old town attracts visitors with the impressive Freiburg Minster and the 'Bächle,' a network of narrow water channels running through the streets. Freiburg is widely known as a 'Green City,' acting as a global pioneer in environmental protection, solar architecture, and sustainability. A large university presence gives the city a youthful and dynamic flair.

Schwarzwald

The Black Forest is Germany's largest continuous low mountain range, located in the southwest of Baden-Württemberg. With its dense pine forests, deep valleys, and picturesque villages, it is the worldwide epitome of German Romanticism. The region is renowned not only for its untouched nature but also for handicraft traditions like the cuckoo clock. As a popular holiday destination, the Black Forest offers excellent hiking trails and outstanding Baden cuisine.
