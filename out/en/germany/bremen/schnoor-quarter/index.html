Schnoor Quarter

View on the map

The Schnoor quarter is Bremen's oldest district, with small fishermen's houses from the 15th century. The narrow alleys and historic half-timbered houses make it one of the best-preserved medieval quarters in northern Germany.

Facts

  • Bremen's oldest district, 15th century.
  • Tiny houses lined up side by side.
  • Today galleries, cafés and crafts.
