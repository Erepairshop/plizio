Bremerhaven

View on the map

Bremerhaven is one of Germany's most significant seaports and Europe's leading location for automobile handling. Ships from all over the world bring new cars here.

Facts

  • Europe's No. 1 for automobile handling.
  • Klimahaus Bremerhaven: exhibition about world climate points.
  • Important location for wind energy components.

Bremen

Bremen, as a city-state, is its own federal state and one of Germany's oldest Hanseatic cities on the Weser River. The city is world-famous through the fairy tale of the Town Musicians of Bremen and has a historic market square with impressive buildings.

Bremen Roland & Town Hall

Bremen's Roland is a 10-meter knight statue on the market square, standing since 1404 as a symbol of freedom and market rights. Together with the Gothic Town Hall, it was declared a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2004.

Schnoor Quarter

The Schnoor quarter is Bremen's oldest district, with small fishermen's houses from the 15th century. The narrow alleys and historic half-timbered houses make it one of the best-preserved medieval quarters in northern Germany.

Bremen's Freimarkt is one of the world's oldest public fairs, held on the Bürgerweide since 1035. The two-week spectacle attracts around four million visitors annually.
