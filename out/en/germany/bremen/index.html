Regions

Bremen

Bremen is the smallest German state, consisting of two cities: the city of Bremen and the coastal port of Bremerhaven. It looks back on a proud history as a free Hanseatic city, reflected in its historic market square and magnificent city hall. Bremerhaven serves as a crucial gateway to the North Sea and a center for the maritime economy. Bremen is world-renowned for the fairy tale of the Town Musicians of Bremen. The city-state's economy is shaped by traditional maritime trade and modern aerospace engineering.

Cities

Bremen

Bremen is a historic Hanseatic city on the Weser River in northwestern Germany. Together with Bremerhaven, it forms the smallest German federal state. The city is world-famous for the fairy tale of the Town Musicians of Bremen and its maritime trading tradition. The historic City Hall and the Roland statue on the market square are UNESCO World Heritage sites, testifying to the city's proud citizenry.
