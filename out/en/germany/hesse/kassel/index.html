Kassel

View on the map

Kassel lies on the Fulda in Hesse and is known for documenta, an international exhibition of contemporary art held every five years. The Wilhelmshöhe mountain park with its palace and Hercules monument is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Facts

  • Home of documenta (every 5 years).
  • Wilhelmshöhe Mountain Park (UNESCO).
  • Wilhelmshöhe Palace with Hercules.

Wasserkuppe

The Wasserkuppe at 950 m is the highest mountain in Hesse and in the Rhön range in central Germany. It is a world-renowned center for gliding and paragliding and offers wide views over the mid-mountain landscape.

Großer Feldberg

The Großer Feldberg at 879 m is the highest peak of the Taunus mountains and of Hesse (excluding Rhön) and lies close to Frankfurt am Main. On clear days the Alps can be seen from its summit, and it hosts numerous transmission facilities.

Frankfurter Paulskirche

The Frankfurt Paulskirche was in 1848/49 the meeting place of the German National Assembly, the first freely elected German parliament, which drafted an all-German constitution. The round church building is considered a symbol of the German democracy movement.
