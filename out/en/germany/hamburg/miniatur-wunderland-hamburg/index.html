Miniatur Wunderland Hamburg

View on the map

Miniatur Wunderland in Hamburg's Speicherstadt is the world's largest model railway and miniature worlds installation. On an area of over 1,500 m², the train runs through landscapes from many countries.

Facts

  • World's largest model railway installation.
  • Over 1,500 m² with landscapes from around the world.
  • Over 15 million visitors since 2001.
