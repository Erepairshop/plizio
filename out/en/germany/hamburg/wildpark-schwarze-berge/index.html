Wildpark Schwarze Berge

View on the map

A large wildlife park in the Harburg Hills featuring an observation tower and flight shows.

Wildpark Schwarze Berge, situated in the Harburg Hills near Hamburg, offers 50 hectares of rugged, natural beauty. Founded in 1969, the park is designed to showcase European wildlife in large, forested enclosures that reflect their natural habitats. Visitors can climb the 45-meter high 'Elbblickturm' for a panoramic view that stretches all the way to the Port of Hamburg. The park is home to over 1,000 animals, including majestic European elks, brown bears, and packs of wolves. Daily flight shows with eagles and falcons provide a thrilling look at these birds of prey in action, making it a key educational site for regional conservation. Sachkunde K4 – European wildlife and forest habitats.

  • Spans 50 hectares of hilly, forested terrain.
  • Features one of Germany's most popular bird of prey shows.
  • Large enclosures house brown bears, wolves, and European elk.
