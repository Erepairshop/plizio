Berlin Zoo

View on the map

Berlin Zoo is Germany's oldest and most species-rich zoo with over 20,000 animals from around 1,500 species. The pandas, gorillas, and elephant park are especially popular.

Facts

  • Germany's oldest zoo (1844).
  • Over 20,000 animals from 1,500 species.
  • Famous pandas and gorillas.

Berlin

Brandenburg Gate

The Brandenburg Gate is Berlin's most famous landmark, built in the 18th century as a neoclassical city gate. During the German division it was a symbol of the Cold War and after reunification in 1990 became a symbol of German unity.

Reichstag Building

The Reichstag Building in Berlin is the seat of the German Bundestag and one of Germany's most significant political buildings. The historic building was completed in 1894 and after reunification was fitted with a modern glass dome by British architect Norman Foster.

Berlin TV Tower

The Berlin TV Tower stands at Alexanderplatz and at 368 m is Germany's tallest structure. It was built in 1969 in East Germany as a symbol of socialism and is today Berlin's most recognizable landmark.

Berliner Mauer

The Berlin Wall was a 155 km fortification that separated West Berlin from East Berlin and East Germany from 1961 to 1989. It stood as a symbol of German division and the Cold War, and its fall on 9 November 1989 was a world-historic event.

Checkpoint Charlie

Checkpoint Charlie was the most famous border crossing between East and West Berlin during Germany's division. Dramatic scenes occurred here, including vehicle break-throughs and a tank confrontation between US and Soviet tanks in October 1961.
