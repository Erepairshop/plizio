Havel (Berlin)

View on the map

The Havel flows through the west of Berlin, forming important local recreational waterways there with Wannsee and Lake Tegel.

Facts

  • Total length 334 km.
  • Flows through Berlin, connecting Lake Tegel and Wannsee.
  • Wannsee is a popular excursion destination and lido.
  • Pfaueninsel (Peacock Island) lies on the Berlin section of the Havel.
  • Part of the federal waterway network.

Brandenburg Gate

The Brandenburg Gate in Berlin is Germany's most famous landmark and a national symbol. It was built in the late 18th century as an early classical triumphal arch. During the Cold War, it stood in the exclusion zone of the Berlin Wall and was a symbol of division. With the fall of the Wall in 1989, it became the emotional emblem of German and European reunification.
