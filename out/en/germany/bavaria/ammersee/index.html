Ammersee

View on the map

Lake Ammersee lies in western Upper Bavaria southwest of Munich and at 47 km² is Bavaria's third-largest lake after Chiemsee and Starnberger See. The Andechs Monastery on the Holy Mountain near the Ammersee is a famous pilgrimage destination and renowned brewery monastery.

Facts

  • Bavaria's third-largest lake (47 km²).
  • Andechs Monastery: famous pilgrimage site.
  • Popular sailing area near Munich.

Chiemsee

Lake Chiemsee is Bavaria's largest lake and is often called the 'Bavarian Sea'. On its islands Herreninsel and Fraueninsel lie historic monasteries and the magnificent Herrenchiemsee Palace, which King Ludwig II had built modeled on Versailles.

Starnberger See

Lake Starnberg lies about 25 km southwest of Munich in Bavaria and is one of the largest lakes in the German Alpine foothills. It is a popular recreation area for Munich residents and known for the mysterious circumstances of King Ludwig II's death in 1886.

München

Nürnberg

Nuremberg is Bavaria's second-largest city and a historically significant imperial city in the heart of Franconia. With its well-preserved medieval castle, it was the venue for Nazi party rallies and after World War II was the site of the Nuremberg Trials.

Regensburg

Regensburg lies on the Danube in Bavaria and is one of Germany's best-preserved medieval cities. The old town with its cathedral, Stone Bridge, and Romanesque towers has been a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2006.

Würzburg

Würzburg lies on the Main in Bavaria and is known for its Baroque townscape, the Würzburg Residence, and the surrounding vineyards of Franconia. The Residence with its court garden and square has been a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1981.
