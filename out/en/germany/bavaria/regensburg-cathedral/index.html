Regensburg Cathedral

View on the map

Regensburg Cathedral of St. Peter is the most important Gothic building in Bavaria and the landmark of the old town, protected as a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2006.

Facts

  • Construction began around 1275, with completion only in the 19th century.
  • The Gothic towers are 105 metres high.
  • The Domspatzen, the cathedral boys' choir, is one of the world's most renowned choirs.
  • Regensburg was recognised as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2006.
  • Nearby stands Germany's oldest surviving stone bridge from the 12th century.

Regensburg

Historical Regensburg in Bavaria is one of the oldest and most beautiful cities in Germany, allowing the Middle Ages to breathe architecturally to this day. With its intact old town, which survived the war almost unscathed, Regensburg is a UNESCO World Heritage site. The Stone Bridge is outstanding, a medieval marvel of engineering. As the former seat of the Perpetual Diet, the city also played a central political role in the Holy Roman Empire for centuries.

Rothenburg ob der Tauber

Rothenburg ob der Tauber, located in Bavaria, embodies the ideal image of a romantic medieval German town worldwide. The almost completely preserved town wall with its gates and defensive towers invites you to take a walk around the old town. Beautiful half-timbered houses, cobblestone streets, and the picturesque Plönlein motif attract countless tourists every year. Despite severe destruction in World War II, the historical center was faithfully rebuilt.
