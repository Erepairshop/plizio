Hellabrunn Zoo Munich

View on the map

Hellabrunn Zoo is one of Germany's largest zoos and the world's first geo-zoo, where animals are grouped by their region of origin. Over 19,000 animals live on 40 hectares on the banks of the Isar.

Facts

  • World's first geo-zoo (1928).
  • Over 19,000 animals on 40 hectares.
  • Located on the banks of the Isar.

Nürnberg

Nuremberg is the second largest city in Bavaria and the historical center of Franconia. The city looks back on a glorious past in the Holy Roman Empire, evidenced by the imposing Imperial Castle above the old town. In the 20th century, Nuremberg became world-famous for the Nazi party rallies and later the Nuremberg Trials. Today, the Christkindlesmarkt, one of the world's most famous Christmas markets, is a major attraction.

Regensburg

Regensburg is located in eastern Bavaria at the northernmost point of the Danube River and is considered one of Germany's best-preserved medieval cities. Its history dates back to the Romans, who built the Castra Regina camp here. The historic center, featuring the famous Stone Bridge and the Gothic St. Peter's Cathedral, is a UNESCO World Heritage site. The old town, characterized by narrow alleys, patrician towers, and an Italian flair, transports visitors back in time. Today, Regensburg is a lively university city with a thriving economy driven by the automotive and electronics industries.

Würzburg

Würzburg is a vibrant university city in Franconia, idyllically surrounded by vineyards on the Main River. The city is the northern starting point of the famous Romantic Road and impresses with a rich cultural heritage. The Würzburg Residence, a masterpiece of Southern German Baroque, is a UNESCO World Heritage Site. A mild climate, traditional wineries, and the Marienberg Fortress shape the character of the city.
