Franconian Jura

View on the map

The Franconian Jura is a limestone mountain range in northern Bavaria with deep river valleys, caves and characteristic Jurassic limestone rocks. It is known for hiking, climbing and beer culture.

Facts

  • Karst landscape with many caves.
  • Altmühltal: nature park and valley hiking paradise.
  • Braujura area: over 200 breweries.
