Bavarian Forest Zoo Lohberg

View on the map

A specialized zoo showcasing the native wildlife of the Bavarian Forest in natural surroundings.

The Bayerwald-Tierpark in Lohberg is the only zoo in the Upper Palatinate, offering a deep dive into the native wildlife of the Bavarian-Bohemian border mountains. Established in 1989, the 10-hectare park focuses exclusively on species that are or were indigenous to the region, providing them with large, naturalistic enclosures. Visitors can observe iconic mountain animals like the Eurasian lynx, wolves, and elks in a setting that mimics their natural habitat. The park plays a crucial role in local conservation and education, showcasing the importance of biodiversity in the Bavarian Forest. It is a quiet, atmospheric alternative to larger commercial zoos, emphasizing the quiet majesty of Central European forests. Sachkunde K3 – Native animals of the forest.

Facts

  • Home to wolves, lynxes, and otters.
  • Educational trail about forest ecology.
  • Focuses on the conservation of native species.
  • Open year-round.
  • Home to approximately 400 animals of 100 different species.
  • The park covers an area of roughly 10 hectares of forest.
  • Specializes in native species like wolves, lynx, and elks.
  • Located in the picturesque Zellertal valley in the Bavarian Forest.
  • Member of the German Zoo Society (Deutsche Tierparkgesellschaft).
  • Founded in 1989 to promote regional wildlife awareness.

Regensburg

Regensburg is located in eastern Bavaria at the northernmost point of the Danube River and is considered one of Germany's best-preserved medieval cities. Its history dates back to the Romans, who built the Castra Regina camp here. The historic center, featuring the famous Stone Bridge and the Gothic St. Peter's Cathedral, is a UNESCO World Heritage site. The old town, characterized by narrow alleys, patrician towers, and an Italian flair, transports visitors back in time. Today, Regensburg is a lively university city with a thriving economy driven by the automotive and electronics industries.
