Obersalzberg – Kehlsteinhaus (Eagle's Nest)

View on the map

Obersalzberg near Berchtesgaden was Adolf Hitler's private mountain residence complex and a power centre of the Third Reich. The Kehlsteinhaus, popularly known as the 'Eagle's Nest', was built in 1938 as a tea pavilion for Hitler and is today a mountain restaurant and tourist destination.

Facts

  • Hitler's private mountain residence 'Berghof' was located here.
  • Kehlsteinhaus built in 1938 as a tea pavilion for Hitler.
  • Today the Obersalzberg Documentation Centre (historic site).
