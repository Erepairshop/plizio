Audi Plant Ingolstadt

View on the map

The headquarters and largest production facility of Audi AG, located in the heart of Bavaria.

The Audi plant in Ingolstadt is the beating heart of the 'Vorsprung durch Technik' brand and its global headquarters. Since Audi's relocation to Ingolstadt in 1949, the site has grown into a massive industrial complex that defines the city's economy. Spanning 2.7 million square meters, it is the second-largest car factory in Europe, producing iconic models like the Audi A3, A4, and A5. The plant is a showcase for modern logistics and manufacturing, featuring a high degree of automation and an integrated rail system that transports thousands of cars daily. Visitors can explore the 'Audi Forum,' which includes a museum and a delivery center, highlighting the brand's journey from historic Auto Union roots to the electric future of the e-tron. Regensburg

Regensburg is located in eastern Bavaria at the northernmost point of the Danube River and is considered one of Germany's best-preserved medieval cities. Its history dates back to the Romans, who built the Castra Regina camp here. The historic center, featuring the famous Stone Bridge and the Gothic St. Peter's Cathedral, is a UNESCO World Heritage site. The old town, characterized by narrow alleys, patrician towers, and an Italian flair, transports visitors back in time. Today, Regensburg is a lively university city with a thriving economy driven by the automotive and electronics industries.
