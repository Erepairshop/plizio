Schloss Neuschwanstein

View on the map

Neuschwanstein Castle is the romantic fairy-tale castle of King Ludwig II of Bavaria, built from 1869 in the Allgäu. It is considered the model for the castle in the Disney logo and is one of the most visited architectural monuments in the world.

Facts

  • Model for the Disney castle.
  • Built from 1869 by King Ludwig II.
  • Over 1 million visitors annually.

Munich's Frauenkirche is the cathedral of the Archdiocese of Munich and Freising with its characteristic onion-dome towers that define Munich's cityscape. The Gothic hall church was built between 1468 and 1488 and is considered the symbol of Bavaria's state capital.
