Wolfspark Werner Freund

View on the map

A unique research center in Merzig where various species of wolves can be observed.

Wolfspark Werner Freund in Merzig is a world-renowned research facility dedicated to the study of wolf behavior. Founded by the legendary researcher Werner Freund in 1977, the park is home to several packs of wolves from different parts of the world, including timber wolves, arctic wolves, and mongolian wolves. Freund's unique method of 'joining' the packs as an alpha member provided unprecedented insights into wolf social structures and communication. Today, the park continues his legacy, offering visitors a rare opportunity to see these misunderstood predators in large, naturalistic enclosures. It serves as a vital center for education, dispelling myths and promoting the conservation of wolves in Europe. Sachkunde K3 – Predator biology and social behavior.

Saarbrücken

Saarbrücken is the state capital of Saarland, located right on the border with France. The city combines a distinct French flair with a past shaped by coal mining and heavy industry. Baroque architecture, designed by master builder Friedrich Joachim Stengel, dominates the cityscape, notably the Ludwigskirche and Saarbrücken Castle. Saarbrücken spans both banks of the Saar River, offering ample green spaces along the waterfront. Today, the city is a modern economic and research hub, driven by Saarland University, which specializes in computer science and artificial intelligence.

Völklinger Hütte

Völklinger Hütte is a former ironworks in Völklingen, Saarland, considered the only fully preserved ironworks from the heyday of industrialization in the 19th and 20th centuries. It received UNESCO World Heritage status in 1994 as the first industrial heritage site.
