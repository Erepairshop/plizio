Bostalsee

View on the map

Bostalsee is a reservoir in Saarland and the region's largest recreational lake. Created in the 1970s, it offers water sports, a beach and nature experiences.

Facts

  • Area: approx. 180 hectares.
  • Most popular excursion destination in Saarland.
  • Water sports: sailing, surfing, swimming.

Saarbrücken

Saarbrücken is the state capital of Saarland, located right on the border with France. The city combines a distinct French flair with a past shaped by coal mining and heavy industry. Baroque architecture, designed by master builder Friedrich Joachim Stengel, dominates the cityscape, notably the Ludwigskirche and Saarbrücken Castle. Saarbrücken spans both banks of the Saar River, offering ample green spaces along the waterfront. Today, the city is a modern economic and research hub, driven by Saarland University, which specializes in computer science and artificial intelligence.

Völklinger Hütte

Völklinger Hütte is a former ironworks in Völklingen, Saarland, considered the only fully preserved ironworks from the heyday of industrialization in the 19th and 20th centuries. It received UNESCO World Heritage status in 1994 as the first industrial heritage site.
