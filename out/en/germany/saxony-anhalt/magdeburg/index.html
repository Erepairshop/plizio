Magdeburg

View on the map

Saxony-Anhalt's capital lies on the Elbe and is home to one of Germany's most significant Romanesque cathedrals. Magdeburg was once the favorite city of Emperor Otto the Great and is the birthplace of physicist Otto von Guericke.

Facts

  • Magdeburg Cathedral (Romanesque).
  • City of Emperor Otto the Great.
  • Birthplace of Otto von Guericke.

Brocken

The Brocken at 1141 m is the highest peak of the Harz mountains and northern Germany and is located in Saxony-Anhalt. It is famous for its often foggy and mystical atmosphere and for Walpurgis Night on April 30th, when according to folklore witches dance here.

Harz

The Harz is Germany's northernmost and highest mid-mountain range, extending across Saxony-Anhalt, Lower Saxony, and Thuringia, with its highest peak the Brocken (1141 m). The range is known for its historic mining towns like Goslar and Clausthal-Zellerfeld, as well as the Harz narrow-gauge railways.

Wittenberg Castle Church

The Castle Church in Wittenberg is the place where Martin Luther is said to have nailed his 95 Theses on 31 October 1517, triggering the Reformation. The event marks the beginning of the Protestant Reformation in Europe.

Lutherstadt Eisleben

Lutherstadt Eisleben is the birthplace and death city of Martin Luther and is located in Saxony-Anhalt. The city with Luther's birth house and death house, as well as the baptismal church St. Peter and Paul, has been a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1996.

Quedlinburg – Collegiate Church

Quedlinburg in Saxony-Anhalt was one of the most important power centres of the Ottonian Empire and has been a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1994. The Romanesque collegiate church of St. Servatius on the castle hill houses the tombs of King Henry I and his wife Mathilde.

Bauhaus Dessau

Bauhaus Dessau is the main building of the famous Bauhaus art school, founded in 1919 in Weimar and moved to Dessau in 1925. The Bauhaus revolutionized architecture, design and art education and continues to influence modern design to this day.
