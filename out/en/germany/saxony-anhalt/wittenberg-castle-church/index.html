Wittenberg Castle Church

View on the map

The Castle Church in Wittenberg is the place where Martin Luther is said to have nailed his 95 Theses on 31 October 1517, triggering the Reformation. The event marks the beginning of the Protestant Reformation in Europe.

Facts

  • 31 Oct 1517: Luther's 95 Theses.
  • Beginning of the Protestant Reformation.
  • UNESCO World Heritage since 1996.
