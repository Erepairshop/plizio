Meissen

View on the map

Meissen is a historic city on the Elbe, known for Europe's oldest porcelain manufactory.

Facts

  • The Meissen Porcelain Manufactory was founded in 1710.
  • Albrechtsburg Castle temporarily housed the secret porcelain formula.
  • The famous Meissen porcelain is called 'white gold'.
