Leipzig – Books & Cars

View on the map

Leipzig is traditionally a book city with Germany's oldest book fair. Today BMW and Porsche have established themselves here, making the city a modern industrial location.

Facts

  • Germany's oldest book fair (since 16th c.).
  • BMW and Porsche operate modern plants here.
  • Important logistics and commercial center.

Dresden

Saxony's capital on the Elbe is considered one of Europe's most beautiful Baroque cities and was once the residence of Saxon electors and kings. Dresden has world-famous sights including the Frauenkirche, the Zwinger, and the Semper Opera House.

Leipzig

Leipzig is Saxony's largest city and was for centuries an important economic center of Central Europe as a trading and fair city. The city is known as the music city of Bach and Mendelssohn, as well as for the Monday demonstrations of 1989 that contributed to German reunification.

Fichtelberg

The Fichtelberg at 1215 m is Saxony's highest peak and lies in the Ore Mountains near the Czech border. It is a popular winter sports center and starting point for hikes through the Ore Mountains, shaped by a mining tradition.

Zwinger

The Dresden Zwinger is a Baroque palace ensemble in Dresden, built by Augustus the Strong in the early 18th century. The Zwinger today houses several important museums, including the Old Masters Picture Gallery with Raphael's Sistine Madonna.

Leipzig Monday demonstrations

The Leipzig Monday demonstrations were weekly protests against the East German regime in autumn 1989, leading to the Peaceful Revolution and the fall of the Berlin Wall. The rally on 9 October 1989 with 70,000 people is considered a turning point in German history.

Leipzig Zoo

Leipzig Zoo is one of Europe's most modern zoos, known for the huge tropical hall Gondwanaland, where gorillas, elephants, and hippos live together in a jungle.
