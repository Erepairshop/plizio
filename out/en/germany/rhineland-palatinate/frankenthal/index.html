Frankenthal

View on the map

Frankenthal is a medium-sized city between Worms and Ludwigshafen.

The two city gates, the Speyerer Tor and the Wormser Tor, are well known. There used to be a famous porcelain manufactory here. Geography K7 — Rivers and Cities

Trier

Trier in Rhineland-Palatinate is proudly the oldest city in Germany, founded by the Romans around 16 BC. In antiquity, 'Augusta Treverorum' was temporarily the capital of the Western Roman Empire and had tens of thousands of inhabitants. Nowhere else in Germany are Roman structures so numerous and well-preserved, foremost the monumental Porta Nigra, the huge amphitheater, and the Imperial Baths. Today, Trier is also known as the birthplace of Karl Marx and as a picturesque wine city on the Moselle river.
