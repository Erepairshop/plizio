Porta Nigra

View on the map

The Porta Nigra is the best-preserved Roman city gate north of the Alps and is located in Trier. The 2nd-century structure is an impressive testimony to Roman urban architecture and has been a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1986.

Facts

  • UNESCO World Heritage since 1986.
  • Best-preserved Roman city gate north of the Alps.
  • In Trier, Germany's oldest city.
