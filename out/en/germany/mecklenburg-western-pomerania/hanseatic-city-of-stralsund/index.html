Hanseatic City of Stralsund

View on the map

Stralsund is a historic Hanseatic city on the Baltic Sea, whose medieval town center with the Town Hall and St Mary's Church has been a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2002. The city was one of the most powerful Hanseatic cities in the 13th–15th centuries.

Facts

  • UNESCO World Heritage since 2002.
  • Major Hanseatic center in the Middle Ages.
  • German Maritime Museum in Katharinenkloster.
