Schwerin Castle

View on the map

Schwerin Castle is one of the most important examples of Historicism in Germany, situated on a lake island.

Facts

  • The castle today serves as the state parliament of Mecklenburg-Western Pomerania.
  • It is surrounded by several gardens and parks in English and French style.
  • The castle is on the UNESCO Tentative List.

Schwerin

Schwerin is the state capital of Mecklenburg-Western Pomerania and the smallest German state capital by population. It is picturesquely embedded in a landscape of lakes, often referred to as the 'City of Seven Lakes.' Its outstanding landmark is Schwerin Castle, which rests majestically on its own island in Lake Schwerin and is considered the Neuschwanstein of the North. The historic old town fascinates visitors with well-preserved half-timbered houses and the Schwerin Cathedral. Despite its small size, Schwerin offers a rich cultural tradition with significant museums and the State Theatre.

Rostock

Rostock is the largest city in Mecklenburg-Western Pomerania and is picturesquely situated on the Warnow River, close to the Baltic Sea coast. The city has a rich past as a powerful member of the Hanseatic League, reflected in the magnificent merchant houses and imposing brick churches of the old town. The University of Rostock, founded in 1419, is one of the oldest in Northern Europe, giving the city a dynamic, student-friendly flair. The Warnemünde district attracts tourists with its wide sandy beaches and historic lighthouse. Economically, port management, cruise tourism, and innovative technologies are also of major importance.

Müritz

Lake Müritz is Germany's largest wholly inland lake (excluding Bodensee) and is located in Mecklenburg-Western Pomerania. It is the centerpiece of Müritz National Park and an important bird sanctuary where cranes and white-tailed eagles rest.
