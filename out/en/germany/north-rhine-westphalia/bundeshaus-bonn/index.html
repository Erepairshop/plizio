Bundeshaus Bonn

View on the map

The Bundeshaus in Bonn was from 1949 to 1999 the seat of the German Bundestag and Bundesrat, making it the heart of West German democracy. After the move to Berlin in 1999, it is now home to Deutsche Welle and various federal authorities.

Facts

  • Seat of the Bundestag 1949–1999.
  • Heart of West German democracy.
  • Today: home of Deutsche Welle.
