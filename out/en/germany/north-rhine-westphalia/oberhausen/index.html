Oberhausen

View on the map

Oberhausen is a city in the Ruhr area in North Rhine-Westphalia and was formerly an important center of the coal and steel industry. The city is known for the Oberhausen Gasometer, a former industrial structure now used as a unique exhibition hall.

Facts

  • Oberhausen Gasometer: unique exhibition hall.
  • Former important coal and steel center.
  • Centro: one of Germany's largest shopping centers.
