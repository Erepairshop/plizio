Phantasialand Brühl

View on the map

Phantasialand in Brühl near Cologne is one of Germany's oldest and most popular theme parks. With spectacular roller coasters, water rides, and elaborate themed worlds, it thrills visitors of all ages.

Facts

  • One of Germany's oldest theme parks.
  • Spectacular roller coasters and themed worlds.
  • Located near Cologne.
