Békéscsaba

Békéscsaba, located in southeastern Hungary, is world-famous for its culinary traditions, particularly the Csabai sausage. The city serves as the cultural center for the Slovak minority in Hungary and charms visitors with its peaceful atmosphere and wide squares. Visitors can explore the Munkácsy Mihály Museum, dedicated to the famous Hungarian painter, or experience the annual Sausage Festival, which attracts thousands of guests. The surrounding area also offers excellent conditions for agriculture and nature tourism.
