Regions

Fejér

Fejér County is located in the heart of Hungary, in the Central Transdanubia region, and is one of the country's most historically significant areas. Its administrative center is Székesfehérvár, the 'City of Kings', which served as the coronation and burial site for Hungarian monarchs during the Middle Ages. The county boasts diverse geographical features, ranging from the picturesque Lake Velence, one of Europe's warmest freshwater lakes, to the rolling hills of the Vértes Mountains. Fejér is now a dynamic economic hub with a strong industrial base, while simultaneously preserving its rich cultural traditions and architectural treasures. Visitors will find a fascinating blend of royal history, modern development, and relaxing natural experiences in this unique region.

Cities

Székesfehérvár

Székesfehérvár, one of Hungary's oldest cities, served as the coronation and burial site for Hungarian kings during the Middle Ages. Located in central Hungary, it is now a major industrial and cultural hub. Visitors can explore the ruins of the coronation basilica in the Ruin Garden or admire the fairytale-like Bory Castle, built by an artist in the 20th century. The Baroque old town, with its monuments and museums, offers a journey through Hungarian history.
