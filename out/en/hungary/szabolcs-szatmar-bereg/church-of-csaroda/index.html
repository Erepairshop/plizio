Church of Csaroda

View on the map

The Reformed church of Csaroda is a late Romanesque gem from the 13th century.

The church is famous for its medieval wall paintings depicting 'smiling saints.' Despite the Reformation, these frescoes were preserved. The church has a characteristic slender tower with wooden shingles.

Facts

  • Late Romanesque style.
  • Frescoes with smiling saints.
  • Wooden shingle tower.
  • Important monument of the Bereg region.
