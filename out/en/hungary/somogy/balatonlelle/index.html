Kaposvár

Kaposvár, the 'City of Flowers and Painters,' is located in southwestern Hungary in the hilly Somogy region. The city is known for its well-kept parks, charming Neoclassical architecture, and rich cultural heritage. Kaposvár is the birthplace of the famous painter József Rippl-Rónai, whose legacy is preserved in the Rippl-Rónai Museum and his former villa. With its university and the renowned Csiky Gergely Theatre, the city serves as a major intellectual hub for the region.
