Győr

Győr, the 'City of Rivers,' is located in northwestern Hungary at the confluence of the Rába, Rábca, and Danube rivers. The city is a major economic hub, home to one of the world's largest Audi engine plants. Its historic center charms visitors with Baroque architecture, cozy alleys, and the majestic Basilica on Káptalan Hill. Győr is also a key transportation hub situated between Budapest, Vienna, and Bratislava.

Sopron

Sopron, the 'Most Loyal City' of Hungary, is located in the far west of the country on the border with Austria. The city is famous for its exceptionally well-preserved medieval old town and its status as a major wine-growing region, particularly for the Kékfrankos (Blaufränkisch) variety. Its landmark is the Fire Tower, offering a magnificent view over the rooftops and surrounding vineyards. Sopron is also a symbol of European unity, as it hosted the Pan-European Picnic in 1989.
